# yashbytes portfolio design system

The interface resembles a hand-drawn paper sketchbook: warm off-white dotted paper, black ink outlines, wobbly borders, tape, thumbtacks, and playful hard shadows.

- Colors: paper `#fdfaf6`, ink `#1a1a1a`, orange `#fb923c`, yellow `#fef08a`, red `#ef4444`, blue `#60a5fa`.
- Typography: Kalam for headings; Patrick Hand for UI/body text.
- Surfaces: white cards on a dotted paper background.
- Edges: `3px` ink borders with irregular hand-drawn border radius.
- Motion: very restrained floating decoration and a pressed hard-shadow card interaction.
- Product media should be real branded images/screenshots, not generic stock photography. Preserve the existing visual style with paper-like framing, tape, and a small thumbtack.
