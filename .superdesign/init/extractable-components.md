# Extractable components

## FeaturedProductCard

- Source: `public/index.html`
- Category: basic
- Description: Hand-drawn polaroid card that presents a product visual, product name, short copy, and a live-site link.
- Extractable props: `name`, `description`, `coverImage`, `screenshots`, `projectUrl`.
- Hardcoded: wobbly border, paper card, tape, thumbtack, handwritten typography, and black hard-shadow treatment.
