how i got here

Started building apps in my first year of college. Three years in, still building. AI was in my toolbox from day one, which makes me a programmer of the GPT era by definition.

It also meant I had to consciously go learn the stuff AI lets you skip: how parsers work, what regex actually does, why boring code wins. I wrote about that in AI slop ruined software engineering. Old-school engineering still beats reaching for an LLM for everything.

Then I proved it to myself. Seven months of wiped expense history, three bank accounts, one offline parser. No model touched the numbers.

what i'm into

AI products people actually use. Not demos, not wrappers around a prompt. Things with users, edge cases, and 2 AM bug reports.

Systems and infrastructure. I daily-drive Arch with Hyprland, run a homelab with VLANs, and automate everything repetitive. I like knowing how the machine actually works, down to the packets.

Networking, as a curiosity, not a title. I've worked through CCNA material for the knowledge, not a certificate. It makes me better at the systems half of my work.
